This gate answers: “Can Praxis provisioning start?”

It confirms that the AWS account, networking, access, ownership, release, and external platform prerequisites are available before DevOps begins setup.

Completion rule: once every applicable item is confirmed, publish and share the setup. The receiving DevOps owner acknowledges the handoff before foundation setup begins.

This is not final environment, application, testing, product-acceptance, or production readiness.

1

Describe the foundation

Capture the environment identity, target account, technical definition, and applicable services.

2

Confirm prerequisites

Record status, owner, and evidence for networking, AWS access, and external dependencies.

3

Publish and share

Publish an immutable readiness snapshot and send its Praxis link to the receiving DevOps owner.

NetworkingCIDR, firewall, TGW, endpoints, internet, VPN, Vault, Harbor, and Rancher paths.
AWS and accessTarget account, region, IAM execution/admin roles, and account availability.
Evidence-backed outputEvery prerequisite has an accountable owner and ticket, link, or verification reference.
Why does this gate ask for these things?Review the Catalyst incidents and canonical Confluence sources behind every Networking and AWS prerequisite.
